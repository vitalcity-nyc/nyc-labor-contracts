NYC municipal labor contracts

Every collective bargaining agreement published by the New York City Office of Labor Relations, in one searchable place. Many of these contracts are published only as scanned image PDFs — the text isn't selectable, you can't even Ctrl-F inside them. This site OCRs every page so the full text of every agreement is searchable, taggable by topic, and quotable with a stable link to the source clause. Methodology & sources
