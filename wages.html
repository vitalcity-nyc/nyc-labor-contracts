A note on these numbers

Curated entries have been verified against the first page of each agreement and reflect compounded general wage increases — the standard NYC bargaining convention. They do not include longevity differentials, recurring increment payments (RIPs), assignment differentials, or title-specific equity adjustments, which can add several percentage points to total compensation growth for some titles.

Lump-sum ratification bonuses ($3,000 in this round) are counted separately because they don't roll into base salary. They're typically pensionable and pro-rated for part-time employees.

The 82 non-curated contracts show only what regex extraction picked up from the first ~8,000 characters of OCR'd text. Treat those as candidates, not facts.
