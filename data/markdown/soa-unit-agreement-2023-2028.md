RE: 2022-2027 Uniform Officers Coalition Economic Agreement ("UOCEA")
Dear Mr. Mannion:
This is to confirm the parties' mutual understanding and agreement concerning additional economic items negotiated by the parties pursuant to Section 4 of the UOCEA ("Unit Bargaining").
1. The term of the agreement set forth in Section 1 of the UOCEA shall be extended by 2
months. Therefore, the term of the Sanitation Officer unit agreement shall be June 25,
2023 through August 24, 2028.
2. Effective June 25, 2026, there shall be an increase of $261 per employee per annum in
the annuity contribution. This amount shall not be increased by future general wage increases.
3. Effective June 25, 2026, there shall be a per annum increase in the welfare fund
contribution of $100 per active employee and retiree.
4. The union shall establish an employee expense reimbursement fund, subject to the terms
of a separate agreement between the parties. The annual uniform allowance payment shall cease beginning with the payment for fiscal year 2025. Effective June 25, 2026, there shall be a $170 per employee per annum increase in the contribution to the employee expense reimbursement fund.
5. Effective June 25, 2026, there shail be an increase of $223 to the 10, 15, and 20-year
longevity payments.

Re:
Agreement covering period from June 25, 2023 to August 24, 2028
Dear Mr. Mannion:
This is to confirm our mutual understanding and agreement that, the Sanitation Officers Association had previously allocated funding to provide a maxinum of seventy-five (75) days of release time with pay and benefits annually, effective April 1, 2003.
This is to further confirm our mutual understanding and agreement that effective June 25, 2026, the Sanitation Officers Association has allocated funds to provide an additional twenty-five (25) days of release time with pay and benefits, thereby allowing a maximum of one hundred (100) such days. The union may carry-over unused days to the following year up to a cap of twenty (20) carried-over days. In the event of extraordinary and unforeseen circumstances that prevent the union from using its release days in a given ycar (e.g., the COVID pandemic), an additional number of days may be carried over at the discretion of the Department.
This is to also confirm our mutual understanding and agreement that the allocation of these days shall be made in the Union's discretion, but shall be subject to the exigencies of the Department and shall require the Union to provide the Department with two weeks' notice in advance of the Union's intent to use such days.
